The Lonko standard

Every Lonko note is built around five questions.

So you always know what you’re reading, how much to trust it, and what to do with it.

1

What did we find?

Observable evidence first, not assumptions.

2

What does it mean?

Technical signals, translated into business meaning.

3

What should you do next?

A practical step, not another metric to interpret.

4

How confident are we?

The strength of the conclusion, and its limits.

5

What’s the evidence?

So the reasoning can be understood and questioned.

And when a note can’t answer one of them with confidence, it says so. A confident answer isn’t always a reliable one.
